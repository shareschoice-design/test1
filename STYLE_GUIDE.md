# Backstory – short-form style guide (fast format)

Goal: the viewer's brain never gets a "safe" moment to swipe away. Real footage + fast explainer visuals.

## Structure (20–35 s)
1. **Hook (0–2 s):** 3–5 word caption that creates a question or threat, last 1–3 words red.
   Examples: `HE CAN'T GO HOME` · `THE DEER CAN'T SEE IT` · `THE LETTER WAS 72 YEARS LATE`.
   The first frame is also the thumbnail – strongest real shot.
2. **Open loop every 5–7 s:** a line that promises more before paying off the previous one.
   - "But that's not the crazy part."
   - "Then something nobody expected happened."
   - "And that's when it went wrong."
   - "Wait for it…"
   Mark these lines with a `label` so they get a hit sound + top text.
3. **Escalation:** each fact bigger/stranger than the last (numbers, stakes, emotion).
4. **Payoff in the last 3 s**, then a line that loops back to the hook so replays feel natural.

## Pacing
- A visual change every ~1.7 s (`CUT=1.7`); never hold one shot longer than 2.5 s.
- Alternate real footage and explainer graphics; real footage at the hook and the payoff.
- Captions: 1–3 words at a time, synced to the voice; 1–2 red keywords per sentence.
- Sentences short: 4–10 words. Cut every word that doesn't add tension or information.

## Emotion triggers (use 2–3 per video)
Threat/danger · time pressure (numbers, countdowns) · injustice · loss → reunion · "you'd never guess" surprise · scale (biggest, fastest, oldest) · identification ("you do this every day").

## Truth rules
- **All footage is real video** (licensed stock, public domain, creator permission). **AI is used only for explainer graphics** (diagrams, maps, cutaways) – never AI-generated people, animals or scenes posing as real footage.
- Real stories only from verifiable sources (kept in a `*_SOURCES.md` file).
- Stock footage illustrates, never pretends to be the real people → "Dramatised with stock footage" in the description.
- Never invent numbers, quotes or dates.

## Audio
- Voice: **en_US-amy-medium** (chosen) for love/people stories, darker male (Ryan, DARK=1) for nature/danger.
- Music ducked under the voice; whoosh on every cut, low hit on every open-loop label.
