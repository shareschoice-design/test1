## Posting rhythm
- 1 short per day, same time (evening in the US: 17:00–19:00 ET ≈ 23:00–01:00 CET)
- First 2 weeks: watch *Average view duration* and *Viewed vs. swiped away*. Over 70 % "viewed" = the hook works.
- Length 30–45 s performs best for this format; loop the ending into the opening.

## Rules that keep the channel safe
1. **Copyright:** BBC / Planet Earth / Nat Geo clips are very likely to get Content ID claims (revenue goes to them) or strikes. Best sources: your own footage, licensed clips, creators who give permission, or free stock (Pexels, Pixabay, Storyblocks).
2. **Reused content policy (monetisation):** voiceover + original explainer visuals + short clips is "transformative", but the channel must look clearly original across all videos – keep the explainer share high (as now).
3. **Credit every creator** in the description, even when you have permission.
4. **AI disclosure:** narration is a synthetic voice and visuals are AI-generated illustrations. They are clearly stylised (not realistic fakes), so YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" label is generally not required – tick it if unsure, it does not hurt reach.
5. **Facts:** keep claims general and verifiable; the dramatised story ("hidden under the snow") is fine as narration, but don't invent numbers.
