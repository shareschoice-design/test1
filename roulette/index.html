Plays evenings of the spins above with your settings, and the same evenings without any waiting, to see if waiting makes a difference.

My rule: when one of your numbers lands, the tracker watches the next N spins for another of them. Each time none comes, that is one miss. After the set number of misses, the next time one of your numbers lands, all of them are bet for one window. Coefficient = −log10 P(this few hits in the last N spins) − log10 P(this long a gap). Higher means the number has been unusually absent. Each spin is still independent: m numbers hit with m/37 (4 numbers 10.8%, 2 numbers 5.4%), and every bet keeps a −2.7% expected value.
