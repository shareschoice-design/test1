How the tracker calculates (and what the maths says)

Cluster = 4 numbers. The chance it hits on any spin is always 4/37 = 10.81%. A corner pays 8:1; a 4-number wheel sector (4 straight-ups at 35:1) also nets 8× the total stake.

Gap = spins since the cluster last hit. P(gap) = (33/37)gap is the chance of a gap that long by luck alone. Once it drops below the trigger, the cluster gets a betting window of 1 to 20 spins: the less likely the gap, the longer the window (log scale between trigger and the full-window level).

Neighbour signal delays the start until the ball lands next to the cluster. Loss-streak brake pauses after a run of lost windows. Max wait makes sure you play at least every N spins: if nothing qualified, it plays the longest-gap cluster in a short window, marked “max wait”.

z-score shows how much a cluster hits more (hot) or less (cold) than expected. Values within ±2 are normal noise on a short sample.

Important: the wheel has no memory. A cluster that missed 40 spins still has exactly 10.81% on the next one. That is why the “chance left” on a bet card comes from 10.81% per spin, not from the gap. Every bet has an expected value of −2.70% of the stake, and no filter, cluster choice or progression changes that. The tracker gives you discipline, limits and an overview; the stress test shows the long-run result. Only play with money you can afford to lose.
