How the picker works (and what the maths says)

You bet 4 straight-up numbers from anywhere, one chip each. A hit pays 35:1 on that number, so the net win is 8× the total stake. The chance that one of 4 numbers lands is always 4/37 = 10.81%.

Filter 1 · Non-hit numbers. Only numbers that have not landed in the last N spins (default 37, about one full “cycle” of the wheel) are candidates. On average 13.4 of 37 numbers miss any 37-spin stretch; the regime filter can wait for stretches with more misses than that.

Filter 2 · Structural spectral analysis. For every number the tracker keeps a running discrete Fourier transform of its hit history (1 = landed, 0 = not) over the last 64/128/256 spins. The strongest frequency gives the number's dominant cycle length; its strength is the peak power divided by the mean power of the spectrum. The phase of that frequency says where the cycle sits on the next spin (1 = at its peak). A number passes when its cycle is strong enough and in phase. Before the history window is full, the filter is warming up and lets numbers through.

Filter 3. Optional hot/cold (z-score) and neighbour signal.

Ranking. Numbers that pass are ranked by score E built from up to four characteristics: γ (gap speed, from P(gap) = (36/37)gap), m (coldness), g (recent landings next to it on the wheel), b (its colour, dozen, column and odd/even behind expectation). When enough numbers pass, the best 4 are bet; if fewer than 4 pass, the best other non-hit numbers fill the set. After the wait limit the best 4 non-hit numbers are bet anyway, so you are never idle for long.

Stakes. Each window starts at the base bet per number. The stake grows by the aggressiveness % every spin and never goes above the max bet. With “every hit in profit” on, the stake is raised when needed so a hit covers all losses of the window plus one base unit, as long as the max bet allows it. The table under the settings shows every spin of a window before you play it.

Always playing. There is no stop-loss, take-profit or pause: the tracker keeps betting until you stop entering numbers.

Important: the wheel has no memory. A number that has missed 150 spins still has exactly 1/37 on the next one. That is why “chance” on the bet card comes from 10.81% per spin, not from the scores. Every bet has an expected value of −2.70%. The Strategy lab compares the picker with random numbers on the same spins so you can check this yourself. Only play with money you can afford to lose.
