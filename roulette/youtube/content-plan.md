# Kitsu Oracle – YouTube content plan

Format of the channel: **"Can a fox spirit predict roulette?"** An anime fox oracle, Kitsu, tries a new "prediction method" every episode on real random spins. Viewers root for her, the scoreboard keeps score. Every result in the videos is genuinely random and unedited.

Put this line at the end of every description:
> Entertainment only. Kitsu plays with play coins on a random wheel. Roulette cannot be predicted; never bet money you can't afford to lose. 18+

Pinned comment idea for every video: **"Which method should Kitsu try next? 👇"** (drives comments, which feed the algorithm).
