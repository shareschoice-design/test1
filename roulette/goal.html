Bold play: each bet is the smallest stake that reaches the target if it wins, or everything you have left if that is less. Small bets give the casino's edge many chances to work: on red or a dozen they rarely reach the target. Long-shot bets lose less this way, but bold play still comes out ahead.

The chances are computed exactly for the bankroll in steps of half the minimum bet, not simulated. Roulette has a negative expected value whatever you do; this plan does not change that, it only gives the highest chance of reaching the target before losing the bankroll. Set the target before you sit down, and leave when you reach it.
